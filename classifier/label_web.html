international — almost never

Only for foreign news with NO Myanmar angle. Of 8,674 gold international articles just 1.6% mention Myanmar. A foreign country acting about Myanmar is not international.

Test: delete every mention of Myanmar — does the story survive? Yes → international. It collapses → label by its subject.

s vs a — not interchangeable

s = not an article (programme announcement, boilerplate). a = a real article the taxonomy can't decide.
Skips are dropped from scoring, so using s on a hard real article removes it from the measurement and flatters the model. Has a byline and prose? Label it or press a — never s.

Label the consequence reported, not the cause

Myanmar's war is the backdrop to nearly everything, so "is the war involved?" never discriminates. Ask what the article actually reports on.
